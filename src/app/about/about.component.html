My story

Read the first letter
of every line

  1. Every door felt closed back then. I was unemployed, with very little money, and I knew exactly how heavy it feels to want to apply for jobs but not have the right tools to move forward.

  2. Looking for help, I struggled to find a CV builder that was truly free, professional, and genuinely useful. Most were locked, limited, or hollow.

  3. Each platform I tried was missing the things that actually matter — real AI assistance, ATS checking, grammar correction, smart suggestions, and proper formatting.

  4. Valuing my time and dignity, I kept trying anyway, sending out application after application, hoping one of them would finally land.

  5. And still I failed, many times, because my CV simply was not strong enough to compete with everyone else in the stack.

  6. The hardest part was knowing it did not present my value clearly — it was not good enough to get me past the very first step.

  7. Out of that frustration, one stubborn idea kept growing: build the honest, powerful tool I desperately wished I had.

  8. Refusing to accept that good tools should only belong to people who can pay, I started building — alone, from nothing, with only determination.

So I built

ELEVATOR

ELEVATOR is the tool I built for the person I used to be — someone who needs a real opportunity but cannot always afford expensive software. My goal is to make professional, AI-powered, ATS-friendly CV creation accessible to everyone.

What I once lacked, I now want to share. If this platform helps even one person feel more prepared, more confident, and one step closer to a better future, then it has already served its purpose.
